SiteWatch

The watch layer.

Every monthly plan includes SiteWatch: the monitoring layer that watches your website between the bigger pieces of work, and reports what it finds as evidence, not adjectives.

  • Automated checks scheduled every 30 minutes.
  • Detects covered failures observed during scheduled checks.
  • Opens an incident and sends an automated notification after two consecutive failed checks, subject to successful notification delivery.
  • Records observed failure and recovery timestamps; actual outage start and end may differ.
  • Reports observed certificate expiration dates and configured expiry warnings.
  • Checks agreed intake routes and configured content markers.
  • Built on the monitoring system DraftSignal uses for its own website.
What you receive

An incident record, and a monthly report

When a check confirms a failure, you get the incident: what failed, the evidence, when it was first observed, and a verified recovery line when a full pass confirms the fix. Quiet periods send nothing.

Every month, the SiteWatch evidence folds into your reporting: on Foundation it lands in your monthly scorecard; Growth Engine adds SiteWatch lines to the weekly report across your full service-page route set; Market Authority adds priority automated incident notification.

SiteWatch detects and reports. Repairs are scoped work under your plan — monitoring never quietly becomes a repair promise.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO means search engine optimization. It focuses on helping search engines understand and discover useful website content. AEO means answer engine optimization, which focuses on making information clear and directly answerable for AI-assisted discovery and other answer-based experiences. DraftSignal addresses search visibility and AI discovery as parts of a business's digital presence. Neither SEO nor AEO guarantees rankings, AI citations, or customer leads.

What digital-presence services does DraftSignal provide?

DraftSignal works on digital visibility, including technical and mobile website health, search visibility, AI discovery, business listings, reviews and reputation, and conversion or lead intake. These are the six areas assessed in its free Construction Visibility Audit. Its paid service plans are Foundation, Growth Engine, and Market Authority. DraftSignal is a digital brand of Apex Grid Engineering PLLC and does not provide engineering services.

Does DraftSignal help businesses improve their websites?

Yes. Website health and conversion are part of DraftSignal's digital-presence work. The free Construction Visibility Audit checks these areas and delivers three verified findings with a 30-day plan. Reporting provides accountability for the work; it does not guarantee a particular commercial result.
