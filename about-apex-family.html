The structure

One family. Separate companies. No blurred lines.

What DraftSignal Digital is

  • A digital services company: SEO/AEO, websites, reputation operations, lead intake and automation
  • Its own brand, contracts, clients, and books — a separate business, run by its own operating team
  • Built exclusively for the construction and design market
  • Run on a continuous operating cadence: monitored daily, reported weekly

What it is not

  • Not Apex Grid Engineering PLLC, and not a department of it
  • Not an engineering firm — it provides no engineering services, seals, or professional approvals of any kind
  • Not a referral channel in disguise — client data never crosses to sister companies without written consent
  • Not a generalist agency — if your business isn't in design, construction, or the trades supply chain, we're not your firm

Why the connection matters — and its limits

Trust signal, not borrowed authority

The Apex name carries weight in this market, and we'd be wasting an asset if we hid the relationship. So we don't hide it — we disclose it, here and in every footer. What the connection gives you is evidence: the systems on our Work & Proof page are real, running, and inspectable.

What it never gives us is engineering authority. If your project needs an engineer, that's a conversation with an engineering firm, under its own name and license — full stop. And if you hire DraftSignal, your data stays yours: no sister company sees your leads, strategy, or pricing without your written consent. The family is a credibility line, not a data pipeline.
