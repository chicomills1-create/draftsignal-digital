The structure

One legal home. Distinct services. No blurred lines.

What DraftSignal Digital is

  • A digital services brand operated by Apex Grid Engineering PLLC: SEO/AEO, websites, reputation operations, lead intake and automation
  • A market-facing brand with its own operating team and service focus; contracts and books remain with Apex Grid Engineering PLLC
  • Built exclusively for the construction and design market
  • Run on a steady operating cadence: checked daily; Foundation reporting is a monthly scorecard, while Growth Engine and Market Authority reporting includes weekly summaries

What it is not

  • Not a separate legal entity or LLC — the legal identity behind the brand is Apex Grid Engineering PLLC
  • Not an engineering-service offering — DraftSignal Digital itself provides no engineering services, seals, or professional approvals of any kind
  • Not a referral channel in disguise — client data is not shared with other Apex-family brands without written consent
  • Not a generalist agency — if your business isn't in design, construction, or the trades supply chain, we're not your firm

Why the connection matters — and its limits

Trust signal, not borrowed authority

The Apex name carries weight in this market, and we'd be wasting an asset if we hid the relationship. So we don't hide it — we disclose it, here and in every footer. What the connection gives you is evidence: the systems on our Work & Proof page are real, running, and inspectable.

What the connection never does is turn a digital-services engagement into engineering authority. If your project needs an engineer, engineering services are provided by Apex Grid Engineering PLLC under its own licenses — full stop. And if you hire DraftSignal, your data stays yours: no other Apex-family brand sees your leads, strategy, or pricing without your written consent. The family is a credibility line, not a data pipeline.
