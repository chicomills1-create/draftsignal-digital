What the audit checks

Six checks. Written findings.

  • Technical & mobile health — mobile performance and rendering, site structure, crawlability, and the technical issues that can hold back discovery
  • Search visibility — how you surface for the work you want, in the markets you serve, by name and by service
  • AI discovery — what the major AI tools say about your firm today, with the queries and dates recorded
  • Google Business Profile & listings — profile completeness, categories, service areas, and whether your listings agree everywhere buyers check
  • Reviews & reputation — review volume, recency, ratings, response posture, and the trust signals a buyer sees before calling
  • Conversion & intake — whether your pages turn visits into inquiries, and whether your forms and contact paths work the way a prospect experiences them

You keep the report either way. If you hire us, it becomes the baseline your plan is measured against. If you don't, you still know where your signal is dark — that's the point of an audit.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does DraftSignal's free Construction Visibility Audit check?

DraftSignal's free Construction Visibility Audit examines six areas: technical and mobile website health, search visibility, AI discovery, Google Business Profile and other listings, reviews and reputation, and conversion and lead intake. The purpose is to identify verifiable issues affecting how a construction or design business can be found and contacted. The audit focuses on evidence, not promises about rankings or future leads.

How long does the free visibility audit take?

DraftSignal delivers the Construction Visibility Audit within two business days. The review covers the same six areas: technical and mobile health, search visibility, AI discovery, business listings, reputation, and conversion or intake. The deliverable is a concise report containing exactly three verified findings and a 30-day action plan. The two-business-day commitment applies to the audit delivery, not to implementation or search performance.

What will I receive from the free audit?

You receive exactly three verified findings and a 30-day action plan. The findings come from a six-part review of your website health, search visibility, AI discovery, business listings, reviews, and lead intake. The plan identifies what to address over the following 30 days. It is a practical assessment, not a guarantee of higher rankings, AI citations, customer inquiries, or booked projects.

Is DraftSignal's visibility audit really free?

Yes. The Construction Visibility Audit is free, with no obligation to purchase a service or start a subscription. You receive three verified findings and a 30-day action plan within two business days. DraftSignal also offers paid digital-presence services, but the audit itself does not require a paid commitment. The findings are intended to help you understand your current digital presence before deciding what to do next.

Who is DraftSignal's free visibility audit designed for?

DraftSignal's Construction Visibility Audit is designed for businesses in construction and related markets, including general contractors, architects, builders, modular manufacturers, drafting and design firms, and trades. It examines how a business appears in search, AI discovery, listings, and reviews, as well as how its website handles potential inquiries.
