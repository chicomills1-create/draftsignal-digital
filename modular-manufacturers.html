What's at stake

Your plant's capacity is invisible if your signal is

Modular wins on speed and certainty, and both are decided in the research phase. A developer comparing three manufacturers will shortlist the ones whose product lines, project history, and service territory are clear in ten minutes — and quietly drop the plant whose website is a brochure from 2015 with a dead contact form.

Meanwhile your competitors are getting cited by AI search tools when buyers ask who builds what, where. That answer layer is being written right now, with or without you in it.

What we build for manufacturers

Product visibility, distributor discovery, routed leads

  • Technical product visibility — product lines, building types, and capabilities structured so search engines and AI tools can cite them accurately: what you build, to what standard, delivered where.
  • Multi-market presence — visibility monitored across every market in your delivery radius, with service-territory pages that carry real substance about real work.
  • Distributor and buyer discovery — findability for the partners who move your product: dealers, developers, and GCs searching by building type and region.
  • Lead routing that respects territories — inquiries routed by geography and product line to the right rep or distributor, with monitored response times, so a hot lead in your territory never lands in a general inbox.
  • Project portfolio development — completed projects documented as they deliver, building a proof library while the work is fresh instead of years later from memory.
