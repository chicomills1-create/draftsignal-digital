FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the difference between DraftSignal's three monthly plans?

Foundation costs $499 per month with a $750 setup fee and includes a monthly scorecard. Growth Engine costs $999 per month with a $1,500 setup fee and includes weekly evidence reporting. Market Authority costs $1,999 per month with a $3,000 setup fee and also includes weekly evidence reporting. All three plans have a three-month initial term, billed monthly in advance. The plan descriptions and agreement determine the work included.

Why does DraftSignal charge a setup fee?

Each DraftSignal plan has a separate setup fee in addition to its monthly subscription. Foundation requires $750, Growth Engine requires $1,500, and Market Authority requires $3,000. The setup fee is not the monthly retainer and should be shown separately wherever prices appear. The precise setup deliverables must be identified in the applicable service agreement rather than implied by the fee alone.

Is there a minimum contract term?

Yes. DraftSignal's Foundation, Growth Engine, and Market Authority plans each have a three-month initial term. Monthly fees are billed in advance during that term. Foundation is $499 per month, Growth Engine is $999, and Market Authority is $1,999, with separate setup fees for each plan. The subscription does not include a guarantee of rankings, AI citations, or leads.

Does the monthly price include advertising or paid directory fees?

No. Advertising spend and paid directories are excluded from DraftSignal's monthly service plans. The published monthly prices are $499 for Foundation, $999 for Growth Engine, and $1,999 for Market Authority, with separate setup fees. Clients should not treat those amounts as covering advertising purchases or paid directory placements. DraftSignal also does not guarantee rankings, AI citations, or leads.

What happens after the initial three-month term?

After the initial three-month term, your plan continues month-to-month at the same rate, billed monthly in advance. You may cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice before your next billing date. No new setup fee applies when your plan continues past the initial term.

What's not included — anywhere

The exclusions, in print

  • Guaranteed rankings, AI citations, leads, or revenue — nobody honest can sell these
  • Advertising spend of any kind (paid search, directories, sponsorships)
  • Third-party subscriptions and tools billed in your name
  • Fake, bought, incentivized, or selectively filtered reviews
  • Fabricated projects, testimonials, or metrics
  • Unlimited revisions or unlimited pages — scope is defined, then honored
How billing works

Simple, in advance, reconcilable

  • Setup fee at signing; plan billed monthly in advance
