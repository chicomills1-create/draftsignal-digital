What's not included — anywhere

The exclusions, in print

  • Guaranteed rankings, AI citations, leads, or revenue — nobody honest can sell these
  • Advertising spend of any kind (paid search, directories, sponsorships)
  • Third-party subscriptions and tools billed in your name
  • Fake, bought, incentivized, or selectively filtered reviews
  • Fabricated projects, testimonials, or metrics
  • Unlimited revisions or unlimited pages — scope is defined, then honored
How billing works

Simple, in advance, reconcilable

  • Setup fee at signing; plan billed monthly in advance
  • Three-month initial term, then month-to-month with a clear cancellation policy
  • Every invoice tied to your account and scope — no mystery charges
  • Weekly evidence reports, so the invoice is never the only proof of work
